03. Financial Ratios & Valuation Cheat Sheet 
[AnalystOS Financial Formula Cheat Sheet]
1. Enterprise Value (EV):
   EV = Market Cap + Total Debt + Preferred Stock + Minority Interest - Cash
2. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):
   WACC = (E/V * Ke) + (D/V * Kd * (1 - Tax Rate))
   - Cost of Equity (Ke) = Rf + Beta * (Rm - Rf) [CAPM]
3. Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF):
   FCFF = EBIT * (1 - Tax Rate) + D&A - Capex - Change in NWC

05. CFA Level 1 Study & Formula Notes 
[CFA Level 1 Equity & Quant concept notes]
1. Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM):
   Ke = Rf + Beta * (Rm - Rf)
2. Dividend Discount Model (DDM) - Gordon Growth:
   Po = D1 / (Ke - g)
3. Dupont Analysis 3-Step:
   ROE = Net Profit Margin * Asset Turnover * Financial Leverage
   ROE = (Net Income / Sales) * (Sales / Assets) * (Assets / Equity)
